Not a medical diagnosis

This is a sound experiment, not a doctor and not a medical device. It matches patterns in the audio. It cannot know what your baby feels, and it can be wrong.

Never use these scores to decide about your baby's health. If your baby seems unwell, is crying in a way that worries you, will not settle, has a fever, or you just feel that something is off, contact your doctor or your local emergency number.

You know your baby better than any page does. Trust that first.
